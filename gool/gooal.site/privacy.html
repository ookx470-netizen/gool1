Privacy Policy

Who we are

GOAL is an investment platform that helps users grow their USDT holdings through structured investment plans with daily returns. We aim to act as a serious, transparent platform.

How the platform works

Deposited funds are placed into structured USDT investment plans and managed through GOAL's trading and investment operations. Daily returns are generated from this activity and credited to your account. This is an investment product — every investment carries risk, and returns vary within the published range for each plan.

Not a pyramid or Ponzi scheme

GOAL is not a pyramid scheme, a Ponzi scheme, or a loss platform. Membership is free — you never pay to join or to invite others. Returns are funded by investment and trading operations, not by recycling new members' deposits. Invitation rewards are paid from platform revenue as a one-time 10% of the plan your invitee activates, and your own earnings never depend on recruiting anyone.

Plan schedule and payouts

Each plan runs for 300 days. Returns accrue on weekdays only — Saturday and Sunday (UTC) are non-earning days. Withdrawal requests are processed within 72 hours to your verified payout wallet, minus the published service fee. An account can only be created after you explicitly accept this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

What we collect

Account details (such as email and display name), session data, investment preferences, deposit and withdrawal records, payout wallet addresses, and operational logs needed to secure and run the service.

How we use information

To authenticate you, deliver the product, process deposits and withdrawals, calculate daily returns, enforce permission controls, improve reliability, and communicate service updates. We do not sell your personal data.

Security

We use encryption for sensitive data, access controls, and operational safeguards. No system is perfectly secure; report concerns through official support channels.

Your choices

You may update preferences, request withdrawals to your verified payout wallet, and contact support for account-related matters subject to applicable law and security checks. Your payout wallet locks after the first save — only an administrator can change it.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the support channel published by GOAL for your region or account.
